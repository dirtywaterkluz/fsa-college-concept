# Partner Model

## FSA Role

FSA would coordinate the concept, promote the pathway, support cohorts, work with sheriff's offices, help align training, support partnership agreements, and advocate for statewide funding.

## College and University Role

Partner institutions would provide academic programs, admissions, advising, credit evaluation, transcripts, certificates, and degrees. Institutions would remain responsible for accreditation, academic policy, student records, and credential conferral.

## Sheriff's Office Role

Sheriff's offices could verify employment, identify workforce needs, encourage participation, provide training records, and support employees through tuition reimbursement or local policy where available.

## State Role

The State of Florida could support the model through legislation, executive direction, state appropriations, tuition waivers, reimbursement mechanisms, or a public safety education trust fund.

## Target Academic Partners

- St. Petersburg College or another Florida College System institution for technical classes, certificates, associate degrees, and selected bachelor's-level public safety programs.
- UCF, USF, and other State University System institutions for bachelor's degree completion pathways.
- FSU, UF, and other graduate institutions for master's degrees, executive leadership, criminology, public administration, correctional leadership, cyber, emergency management, and public safety policy.
- Other universities for specialized graduate and executive education programs.

## Future Implementation Items

- Partner selection and program mapping.
- Admissions and advising workflows.
- Data sharing and privacy agreements.
- Credit review procedures.
- Cohort scheduling.
- Tuition and reimbursement processes.
- Communications and agency onboarding.
