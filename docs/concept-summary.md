# Florida Sheriffs College and Training Institute Concept Summary

## Working Title

Florida Sheriffs College and Training Institute

## Tagline

A statewide education and training pathway for Florida's sheriff's office professionals.

## Core Concept

The Florida Sheriffs Association would host and coordinate a statewide education and training pathway for sheriff's office employees and, in future phases, other Florida public safety professionals.

FSA would not award degrees. Degree-granting colleges and universities would provide academic programs, process admissions, evaluate credit, maintain transcripts, award certificates, and confer degrees.

## Core Message

FSA creates the front door. State colleges and universities provide the academic engine. Sheriff's offices provide the workforce. The State of Florida can help create the long-term funding structure.

## Intended Audience

The concept is designed for presentation to FSA leadership, sheriffs, state college partners, university partners, state leaders, and potential legislative supporters.

## Strategic Value

- Creates one coordinated entry point for sheriff's office employees.
- Connects technical training, certificates, associate degrees, bachelor's degrees, graduate programs, and executive education.
- Helps sheriff's offices support recruitment, retention, career mobility, and leadership development.
- Gives academic partners a statewide public safety audience.
- Provides state leaders a scalable workforce development model.

## Implementation Posture

This is a concept, not an approved college or final program. Admissions, credit, tuition, reimbursement, governance, data sharing, and partnership terms would be addressed through implementation planning and partner agreements.
