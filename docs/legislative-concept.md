# Legislative and Executive Support Concept

## Concept Language

Florida has an opportunity to create a statewide public safety education compact that supports recruitment, retention, leadership development, and professionalization across law enforcement, corrections, detention, probation, and related public safety fields.

## Policy Purpose

Legislation or executive direction could require or encourage public institutions to participate in a statewide law enforcement and public safety education pathway. The pathway could help eligible public safety employees access technical training, certificates, degrees, and leadership development through coordinated public college and university partnerships.

## Potential State Actions

- Encourage Florida College System and State University System participation.
- Support transfer and pathway alignment.
- Create or fund tuition assistance.
- Establish a reimbursement model for participating institutions.
- Authorize a public safety education trust fund.
- Support statewide reporting on participation and outcomes.
- Encourage recognition of prior public safety training where consistent with institutional policy.

## Implementation Posture

The concept should not overstate what legislation would immediately solve. Academic authority, accreditation, admissions, credit evaluation, and degree conferral would remain with partner institutions. Funding and governance details would require additional work.
