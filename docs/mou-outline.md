# Memorandum of Understanding Outline

## 1. Parties

Identify FSA, participating academic partner, and any participating sheriff's offices or state partners.

## 2. Purpose

Describe the coordinated education and training pathway for eligible sheriff's office employees and future public safety participants.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

Define FSA coordination, partner institution academic responsibilities, agency verification responsibilities, and any state support role.

## 4. Academic Authority

Confirm that partner institutions retain authority over admissions, advising, curriculum, academic credit, transcripts, certificates, degrees, accreditation, and student records.

## 5. Pathway Programs

List technical training, certificates, associate degrees, bachelor's programs, graduate programs, executive education, or other offerings included in the partnership.

## 6. Prior Training Review

Describe how employees may submit training records for possible credit review, subject to partner institution approval and institutional policy.

## 7. Tuition and Funding

Describe tuition rates, cohort pricing, scholarships, reimbursement, grants, state support, or other affordability mechanisms.

## 8. Data and Privacy

Address student records, employment verification, data sharing, FERPA, agency records, and reporting requirements.

## 9. Communications

Define approved public messaging, branding, disclaimers, recruitment materials, and points of contact.

## 10. Term and Termination

Set the MOU term, renewal process, review schedule, and termination provisions.

## 11. Implementation Plan

Attach a launch timeline, responsible parties, pilot cohort plan, reporting plan, and issue-resolution process.
