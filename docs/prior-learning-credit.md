# Prior Learning Credit Concept

## Purpose

The concept would help eligible employees gather and submit prior training records to partner institutions for possible credit review.

## Careful Language

- Possible credit review.
- Subject to partner institution approval.
- Not guaranteed.
- Based on institutional policy.

## Examples for Possible Review

- Academy training.
- CJSTC certification.
- Law enforcement certification.
- Corrections certification.
- Dual certification.
- FTO training.
- Instructor training.
- Leadership academies.
- FSA training.
- NIC training.
- AJA training.
- Other approved professional development.

## Partner Institution Authority

Partner institutions would decide whether prior training qualifies for credit, how much credit may apply, and how credit fits within a certificate or degree program. FSA could help organize records and communicate the process, but it would not award academic credit.
